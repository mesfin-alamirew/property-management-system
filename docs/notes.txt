| ID            | Business Rule                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **BR-PV-001** | Only an authorized administrative user may create a Physical Verification session.                                                                               |
| **BR-PV-002** | A verification session has a defined scope, and its asset list is captured as verification items when the session is initiated.                                  |
| **BR-PV-003** | Assets are generated from the scope and may be adjusted before the session starts. Once started, the verification item list is frozen.                           |
| **BR-PV-004** | A verifier may record a physically observed asset that is not registered in the system. It does not automatically create an Asset record.                        |
| **BR-PV-005** | A completed verification cannot be directly modified. Corrections require an authorized correction/review process that preserves the original record and reason. |

4. One more useful discovery

Your AcquisitionItem contains:

assetId String @unique

This means your current model enforces:

One Asset can appear in only one AcquisitionItem.

That may be exactly what we want if an asset represents a unique physical asset and its acquisition record is immutable.

But we should eventually discuss this rule because an asset can theoretically have acquisition-related adjustments or multiple acquisition records depending on the organization's business process.

We don't need to change it now. I'm just flagging it as an architectural decision worth recording in docs/notes.txt / the eventual PMS handbook.

| Scope                          | Short Description                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **ORGANIZATION**               | Verifies **all registered assets belonging to the entire organization**, regardless of organization unit or location.                                                                         |
| **ORGANIZATION_UNIT**          | Verifies **all assets assigned to employees belonging to a specific organization unit**.                                                                                                      |
| **LOCATION**                   | Verifies **all assets physically registered at a specific location**, regardless of which organization unit they belong to.                                                                   |
| **ORGANIZATION_UNIT_LOCATION** | Verifies **all assets at a specific location that belongs to a specific organization unit**. This is the most restrictive scope based on both organizational ownership and physical location. |
| **SELECTED_ASSETS**            | Verifies **only specifically selected assets**, regardless of their organization unit or location. Useful for targeted or special-purpose verification.                                       |
